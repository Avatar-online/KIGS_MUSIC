WHAT IS KIGS MUSIC

KIGS MUSIC

KIGS MUSIC is an independent music label and creative platform from Haryana, India. We work with emerging and independent artists to release, showcase and promote original music across Hindi, Punjabi, Urdu and other sounds. Our focus is on emotional, melodic and modern music, giving artists a platform to share their work and connect with listeners.
